# Introduction to CTFs

Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions are exercises in which "flags" are secreted in deliberately vulnerable programs or websites.

## What is a Flag?

A flag is usually a string of text that looks like this: `CTF{this_is_a_flag}`.

## Types of Challenges

1. **Jeopardy-style**: Teams solve independent challenges in various categories.
2. **Attack-Defense**: Teams defend their own network while attacking others.

## Categories

- **Web**: Exploiting web applications.
- **Crypto**: Breaking encryption.
- **Pwn**: Binary exploitation.
- **Reverse Engineering**: Analyzing compiled code.
- **Forensics**: Analyzing digital evidence.
