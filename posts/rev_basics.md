# Reverse Engineering Basics

Reverse engineering is the art of understanding how a system works by analyzing it.

## Tools of the Trade

- **Ghidra**: A software reverse engineering (SRE) suite of tools developed by NSA.
- **IDA Pro**: The interactive disassembler.
- **GDB**: The GNU Project Debugger.

## Assembly 101

Understanding x86/x64 assembly is crucial.
